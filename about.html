Since 1969

Beauty Shopping Felt Like Discovery

Sephora started as a single, curious idea: what if beauty shopping felt like discovery instead of a decision? Since 1969, that open-sell, test-it-yourself philosophy has grown into a global beauty destination that carries hundreds of brands — prestige, indie, and our own Sephora Collection — under one roof.

Walk into any Sephora and you can touch, swirl, spritz and sample everything on the shelf. No pressure, no gatekeeping, just the freedom to find what makes you feel like you.

Our Journey

Fifty Years Of Firsts

1969

A Perfumery Is Born

Dominique Mandonnaud opens a small perfumery in Limoges, France, built on a simple idea: let customers choose for themselves.

1970s

Open-Sell Revolution

Shelves open up so customers can try fragrance and makeup freely, changing how the world shops for beauty.

1990s

Going Global

Sephora grows across Europe and beyond, bringing prestige and emerging brands together in one space.

2007

Beauty Insider Begins

A free rewards programme turns every purchase into points, perks and personal recognition.

Today

Beauty For Everyone

Online, in-store and on every screen, we make it easier than ever to find shades, scents and routines that feel like you.

Our Mission

Beauty Is Personal, Playful, And For Everyone

Our mission is to make it effortless for every customer to find products that work for them, guided by expert curation rather than guesswork.
